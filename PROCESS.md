# AI tools used
I initially came up with two core arguments for my essay but failed to develop a third complete viewpoint. Therefore, I used AI for brainstorming to enrich my essay structure. I also asked AI to search for relevant design cases and academic papers to support my arguments. In addition, I utilized AI to translate and expand my original content while preserving my core ideas. Furthermore, AI assisted me in reviewing and understanding the knowledge from the last class, including the basic meaning of code, how to clone and upload repositories, the installation and use of Git and Python, and the standard writing rules of Markdown.

# One thing I kept from AI
I know how to construct and argue a clear viewpoint, but my limited writing accumulation often makes my sentences plain and insufficiently refined, weakening the persuasiveness of my arguments. AI perfectly compensates for this shortcoming. In this assignment, I first sorted out a complete writing framework and clarified my core themes, such as the positive impacts of programming on designers. Then I used AI to enrich sentence expressions and expand the full text. This is the part I kept from AI.

# One thing I rejected from AI
After AI completed the translation and content expansion of my essay, I rejected the original revised version generated by AI. I found that the AI’s initial content lacked sufficient persuasive reasoning and loose argument logic. To optimize the essay structure, I collected several standard argumentative essays and let AI learn the rigorous writing logic of proposing core viewpoints, demonstrating with specific cases, and summarizing and echoing the thesis. I then asked AI to revise the full text according to this standardized argument structure to make the reasoning more solid and hierarchical.
