# Why are we here

With the rapid development of artificial intelligence, current AI tools can generate usable program code with simple instructions, significantly lowering the technical threshold of digital creation and improving creative efficiency. This has sparked a debate: now that artificial intelligence can quickly handle basic programming work, do designers still need to systematically learn programming? In my opinion, designers need to learn programming. **Therefore, I argue that designers need to learn programming.**

## Learning programming helps designers understand underlying logic and raises their creative ceiling.

Ordinary designers rely on AI visualization tools and templates and can only produce standardized dynamic effects, as modern AI design tools rely on convergent training data and fixed generation logic, inevitably producing homogeneous and formulaic design outputs (*[Lega Design, 2026](https://www.lega.design/blog/ai-design-tools-2026-why-everything-looks-the-same)*). In contrast, new media designers who master programming and underlying medium logic can independently write particle systems, interactive rules and dynamic algorithms. They break the preset limitations of mainstream tools and create unique generative art and customized interactive works. For instance, most commercial AI design tools only provide fixed dynamic transition effects for web and interface design. Designers with programming skills can customize exclusive interactive logic, dynamic feedback effects and visual presentation rules tailored to brand characteristics and project demands. Such personalized design results cannot be achieved by simple AI template generation, effectively helping designers get rid of homogeneous design and greatly expanding their creative boundaries and professional potential. Therefore, learning programming is an essential way for designers to break tool limitations and elevate their upper creative limit.

## Learning programming enables better communication with development teams and reduces creative loss.

According to *[How AI Programming Reshapes Designers’ Workflow](https://cloud.tencent.com/developer/article/2645047)*, designers with coding knowledge can reduce project revision rates by 67% when collaborating with developers, maximising the preservation of original design ideas. This means that designers with programming foundations can accurately judge technical feasibility, describe design rules with developer-friendly logical language, and precisely convey interactive details to improve overall development efficiency. Therefore, learning programming is extremely necessary for designers

## The history of technological evolution proves that only creators who master their medium can innovate.
In the 19th century, with the birth of photography, the public believed that accurate and realistic photographic technology would completely replace realistic painting. However, outstanding artists did not resist the new technology. Instead, they actively learned and understood the optical principles and visual logic of photography, and integrated the new medium into painting creation. Impressionist painters drew on photography’s instantaneous composition, dynamic framing and light observation methods, breaking the rigid realistic paradigm of classical painting and establishing a brand-new light and shadow art system. Therefore, learning programming allows designers to better grasp medium principles, embrace new tools, adapt to technological iteration, and achieve continuous innovative creation.

Photography was once expected to replace painting, but outstanding artists took the initiative to learn and adopt the new medium. For instance...**This is why designers should learn programming.**

In conclusion, for designers, learning programming can not only **improve personal design ability**, but also help **improve team efficiency** and **grasp the essence of innovation**. Therefore, learning programming brings numerous benefits to designers, which is exactly why we are here to learn programming.
